Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I book a visit?

Open any home and choose “Book a visit”. Share a preferred date and your contact details, and we'll confirm a time within one working day.

Are the listings verified?

Listings are managed by our team with details supplied for this demo. The sample homes shown here are demo content, not verified live inventory.

How do I list my home?

Send us a note with your address, expected rent, and a few photos. We'll reply within a working day to arrange a visit and prepare the listing.

What does the deposit cover?

Deposits are shown on each home and are typically two months' rent. The rental agreement sets out the refund terms before you sign.
